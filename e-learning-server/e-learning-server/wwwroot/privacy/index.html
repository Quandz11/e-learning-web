Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026 | Version 1.0.0
Core Commitment: The Nhịn Ăn Chữa Lành mobile application and platform are dedicated to protecting your privacy. Your wellness and fasting data are securely encrypted. We NEVER sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties for commercial or advertising purposes.

1. Information We Collect

To provide personalized fasting guidance and wellness learning, we collect the following:

  • Account Data: Full name, email address, phone number, and optional avatar image.
  • Fasting & Wellness Log: Fasting schedules, hydration logs, and daily wellness self-assessment entries.
  • Learning Progress: Completed lesson parts, video watch durations, and quiz evaluation scores.
  • Device Information: Operating system version and device model to deliver push notifications and mobile home widgets.

2. How We Use Information

Your data is used solely to:

  • Provide personalized fasting guidance and circadian biological schedule reminders.
  • Maintain educational course progress and completion status.
  • Facilitate secure account authentication (OTP, 2FA) and provide customer support.

3. Data Security & Storage

  • All network communication is strictly encrypted via standard HTTPS / TLS protocols.
  • Session tokens are stored inside hardware-backed secure storage (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain).
  • Our backend systems enforce strict multi-layer authentication and firewall restrictions.

4. User Rights & Account Deletion

You retain full ownership and control over your personal data:

  • Access & Update: Modify your personal profile in the app at any time.
  • Permanent Deletion: You can delete your account directly in the mobile app under Settings & Security > Delete Account, or online at our deletion portal: nhinanchualanh.vn/delete-account. Upon deletion, all personal data is permanently erased.
